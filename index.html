About Me

I am Andrea Rocca, a mathematician currently completing my Master's degree in Mathematics at the University of Pisa. Starting from the next academic year, I will be pursuing my PhD in Mathematics at GSSI (Gran Sasso Science Institute), where I ranked 1st in the admission call.

My research interests focus on regularity theory in PDEs via variational techniques. During my studies, I have developed a strong expertise in sharp functional inequalities, shape optimization, and free boundary problems. I am particularly interested in understanding the regularity properties of solutions to nonlinear PDEs and their connections to optimization problems. I also spent a semester as a Visiting Student at the Universitat of Barcelona, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Xavier Ros Oton.
